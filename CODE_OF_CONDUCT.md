# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Disagree about the work,
not the person.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other space this project occupies.

## Expected

- Take criticism of your code as criticism of your code.
- Explain the *why* when you disagree; "this is wrong" is not a review.
- Respect that some things here are deliberate refusals, not gaps. If you think
  a refusal is wrong, argue the reasoning — see
  [ADR-0008](docs/adr/0008-owner-restriction-policy.md) for the one that comes
  up most.
- Nobody owes you a fix on your schedule.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualised content,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing someone's private
information, or persistently pushing this project toward password cracking or
DRM circumvention after being told no.

## A note specific to this project

This is a tool for unlocking files you have the password for. Requests to turn
it into a password recovery tool, a DRM stripper, or a restriction bypass will
be declined on the merits, every time. Repeatedly re-filing them, or filing
them with a story about why your case is different, is disruption rather than
contribution.

## Reporting

Email **jayanthreddy268.jr@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. Include
what happened, where, and links if there are any.

Responses range from a request to change behaviour, through removing a comment,
to a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern. The maintainer decides,
and will say what was decided and roughly why.
